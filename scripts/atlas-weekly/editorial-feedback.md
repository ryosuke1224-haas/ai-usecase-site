# Owner feedback for editorial calibration

Give practical opportunities and the excitement of building with AI room to lead. Governance, validation, and risk matter where the stakes justify them. Troubleshooting stories are stronger when the underlying error, reproduction, and verified fix can be safely shared.

Add owner-approved, public-safe article excerpts and a brief explanation of what each does well below. Use examples to calibrate judgment and never as templates or as factual evidence for a different article.

## When an enabling technique takes over the story

The owner reviewed an analytics article that turned a promising idea about reusable AI analytics environments into a customer-count reconciliation procedure. The procedure became more accurate and specific through revision, but the article still left the reader with a much smaller idea.

The original opportunity was to build an environment that accumulates historical analysis, validated SQL and analytical methods, business definitions and rules, approved results, organizational knowledge, and previous decisions. That accumulated work could support recurring analysis, scenario simulation, proactive insights, and progressively more delegated analytical work where evidence, permissions, and review justify it. Baseline reconciliation can make that environment trustworthy. Give it the lead when the reader's best opportunity is the technique itself.

Compare what readers could discover, build, or decide under different angles before committing to a thesis. A narrow technical or governance story can be the strongest choice when it solves a consequential problem. This example is calibration for editorial judgment, with no required topic, structure, or factual claims for other articles.

## Give discovery its own editorial value

The owner wants Atlas to actively cover new AI products, tools, and capabilities. A useful story may help a reader understand or try one new capability, compare it with their current workflow, or see a concrete business opportunity. It can focus on the practical implications most relevant to that story. Verify availability, pricing, and technical capabilities where relevant, and make clear when an application or business opportunity is hypothetical.
